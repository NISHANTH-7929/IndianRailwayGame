# S4 inspection and bounded design

Read accepted S2/S3 reports and actual kernel, handling, tests, subsystem and widget before modifications. Their snapshots/history are authoritative. Latest S4 request requires prevention of any unauthorized crossing; this supersedes the older roadmap's optional physical-overrun failure response. No AI or gameplay systems are authorized.

Keep the seven-edge topology and geometry unchanged. Train A is the existing181m formation on E1. Train B is the43m formation on E2, approaching the common E7 exit. Stable block definitions partition all edges; occupancy intersects the complete path interval [head−formationLength,head] with directed occurrences, including gaps conservatively. Swept old/new intervals are checked as well, so intermediate crossings cannot disappear.

The small terminal edge cannot park both formations. Define explicit train-assigned holding areas (A's E1 approach and E7 beyond10m; B's E2 approach). These are immutable fixture allocations, not shared resources silently omitted from protection. Route A reserves the split, upper branch and shared merge; route B reserves the lower approach and merge, ending5m into E7. Only A can enter its terminal holding area. This permits A's rear to clear the shared merge while A remains parked, then B can obtain its route without overlap. Document this conservative fixture restriction; it is not a general railway berth allocator or signalling rule.

Use separate immutable block/point/route/signal definitions and mutable occupancy, route locks, point locks, authority and derived aspect records. Grant transactions copy/validate all resources before commit. Owner tokens include route, train and grant generation. Release requires all reserved blocks clear of the train after entry, or a safe pre-entry cancellation; stale tokens cannot release a newer grant. Point movement also checks physical occupancy independent of route ownership.

Extract the existing accumulator loop into one reusable pure fixed-step clock, keeping30Hz/four-step/debt-retention unchanged. The S4 world owns that clock and multiple S2/S3 train states; the subsystem remains the sole mutable Unreal owner. S2/S3 modes retain their behaviour. Each S4 step uses a candidate world state: process queued requests, derive permission, submit conservative braking commands through S3, step every train once, validate swept movement/authority, recompute occupancy, release eligible resources, derive signals, validate invariants, then commit. A guard violation rejects the whole step and enters an observable diagnostic pause with physical speed/position preserved, not a teleport or instantaneous physical stop. Normal restrictive approaches use S3 brake response before that hard limit.

Route grant commits the permitted path through S2 history extension; neither points nor signals rewrite history. Missing/invalid operational state denies movement. Signal and point Actors will be a disposable read-only view; actual destroy/recreate evidence is required in Unreal/Android in addition to pure snapshot tests.

Implement pure operations and negative/invariant tests first. Then add small route request controls and debug presentation, full S2/S3 regressions, Unreal Automation, wireless Android interaction/lifecycle, opt-in profiling and an uninterrupted default-launch run. Preserve failures and hashes. STOP after S4, before S5.
