# Station candidate visual review

Latest fittings revision: 11 Egmore modules, including bench, water taps, bins,
platform board, drain and ceiling fan. Updated imported LOD totals:
69,433 / 38,187 / 17,354. The platform-level preview now has readable-facing
fixtures and rear drainage clear of the safety marking. Named Rotator arguments
fixed board orientation and the daylight direction. These additions pass static
validation but are not Android-measured or accepted as final realistic art.

2026-10-04: V6 phone images supersede the V5 platform-placement images. See
`Evidence/Station/Device/station-v6-a/screen-47.png`: the platform is now beside
the train after the FBX axis correction. Debug signals, sparse surroundings,
the cyan AI proxy and uniform surfaces still prevent realism acceptance.

The separate Egmore study has revised windows, doors, open portico, relief and
daylight previews at `Evidence/Egmore/unreal-study.png` and
`Evidence/Egmore/unreal-canopy-study.png`. These are Unreal editor images, not
phone captures. The façade is an estimated modular study, not the complete
Egmore layout. Remaining work includes accurate pavilion proportions, further
ornament, Tamil/Hindi signage, station-specific platform planning, localized
weathering, lighting beneath the roof, interior depth and everyday platform
fittings. The new Egmore source totals 59,067 triangles across five reusable
modules; imported LOD totals are 58,909 / 32,401 / 14,726.

The independently authored kit is a functional candidate, not yet accepted as
matching the requested high-realism reference quality. Public marketplace preview
images are comparison evidence only; no source textures, topology or UVs were used.

| Reference feature | Current candidate | Remaining work |
|---|---|---|
| Concrete footbridge stairs | Closed stepped slab and inclined underside; thin rails | Landings, supports and edge wear need closer review |
| Roofed steel footbridge | Reusable six-metre deck, verticals and diagonal rails | Roof seams, fasteners, localized weathering |
| Platform canopy | Double-member columns, splice plates, bolts, trusses, corrugated roof | Mobile-distance aliasing and finer construction detail |
| Platform paving and coping | Modular surface, yellow line, rail-relative height and edge offset | Less uniform wear and dirt at joints |
| Yellow station boards | Independent text geometry on both faces | Further lettering and local-language reference research |
| OHE and cabinets | Mast, portal, insulators, cantilever, contact wire, cabinet | Full support/grade alignment and additional small fittings |

| Adjoining scene | Ground tiles and boundary | Ground transitions, drainage and restrained vegetation |
| Signals | Existing authoritative debug indicators remain | Proper housings and visible lamps driven by existing aspects |

`Evidence/Station/assembled-blender.png` verifies assembly in the source scene.
`Evidence/Station/Device/station-v5-a/screen-80.png` shows the actual Android
exterior camera after moving it clear of the platform canopy. These are different
renderers and views; the Blender image is not evidence of Android rendering.

Architectural measurements other than the documented gauge/platform constraints
are estimates. This kit does not certify prototype construction or clearance.
The surrounding flat ground and debug signals remain conspicuous. Consequently,
successful imports, tests and frame-rate measurements must not be described as
completion of all S8 art, weather, sound or environment work.
