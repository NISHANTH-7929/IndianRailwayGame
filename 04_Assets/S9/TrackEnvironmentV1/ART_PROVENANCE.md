# Track and environment texture provenance

Created 25 October 2026 with the built-in imagegen tool under the imagegen skill.
These are generated material images, not photographs or surveyed route evidence.
Both selected source images are copied unmodified into Textures in this folder.
The full generation prompts are retained in generation-prompts.json.

- T_TrackBallast_BC.png: angular grey granite ballast, overhead, diffuse light.
- T_DryGround_BC.png: compacted dry dusty earth, fine gravel and sparse straw.

Material scale and appearance are checked in Unreal. No accuracy claim is made
about aggregate mineralogy, exact prototype grading, or measured ground colour.
