GAME NAME:
Working title: Indian Railway

PLATFORM:
Android

ENGINE:
Unreal Engine 5

CORE IDEA:
A realistic, living Indian railway experience.

CORE EXPERIENCE:
The player operates an Indian train while the railway
around them behaves dynamically.

TARGET PLAYERS:
- Casual mobile players
- Indian railway enthusiasts
- Train simulation enthusiasts
- Railfans

CORE GAMEPLAY:
Drive → Observe signals → Manage speed → Stop at stations
→ Interact with railway traffic → Complete objectives
→ Earn rewards → Unlock content

KEY DIFFERENTIATOR:
A living Indian railway rather than simply a train-driving game.

MVP:
A proper, polished and enjoyable game with enough trains,
routes, stations and gameplay variety to feel complete,
while advanced simulation systems can be added later.

LONG-TERM VISION:
A large interconnected Indian railway simulation with
dynamic traffic, realistic operations, authentic trains,
stations, environments and events.
